Our Story

A table built
on two worlds

Harbour & Vine was born from a simple obsession — to prove that Papua New Guinea's extraordinary produce deserves a world-class kitchen. Our chefs work directly with local fishermen, highland farmers, and coastal growers to build every menu around what's freshest and most honest.

The result is something entirely our own. Modern technique applied to Pacific soul. The kind of food that makes you feel like you're discovering PNG for the first time.

"We don't import the Pacific. We cook it."
— Marcus Teine, Head Chef & Co-Founder

The Experience

More than
a meal

Harbour & Vine is designed for moments worth remembering — from first dates to long business lunches to private celebrations overlooking the harbour.

Harbour View Dining

Every table in our main dining room looks out across Ela Beach and the Coral Sea — best at sunset with a glass of our house Riesling.

Private Events & Functions

Our private dining room seats up to 24 guests — perfect for corporate functions, celebrations, or an exclusive chef's table experience.

Wine & Cocktail Program
