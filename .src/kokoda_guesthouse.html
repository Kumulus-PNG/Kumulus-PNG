Our Story

Born from the land,
built for the journey

Kokoda Ridge was founded by a local PNG family with one vision — to share the breathtaking beauty of the Highlands with the world, without losing what makes it sacred.

Every bungalow is hand-built using traditional materials. Every meal is sourced from our garden or local village markets. Every guide is a community member who has walked these trails since childhood.

This is not a resort. This is Papua New Guinea — raw, real, and unforgettable.

Experiences

Beyond the guesthouse

Every day at Kokoda Ridge is an opportunity to connect with PNG's extraordinary natural and cultural heritage.

🥾

Kokoda Trail Trekking

Guided multi-day treks through the historic Kokoda Trail with experienced local guides who know every ridge and river crossing.

🦜

Bird of Paradise Tours

Dawn wildlife walks to observe PNG's iconic Birds of Paradise in their natural highland habitat — a once-in-a-lifetime experience.

🏺

Village Cultural Tours

Spend a day with our neighbouring village community — witness traditional sing-sings, learn crafts, and share a local meal.

🌿

Rainforest Canopy Walk

Walk among the treetops on our suspended canopy bridge system, built with local timber and offering views across the valley.
